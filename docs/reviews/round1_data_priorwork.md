## A. Data findings (facts)
- Keys: CRISPRGeneEffect (1,361 models) and Omics files share ModelID (every omics SequencingID is in OmicsProfiles, every ModelID in Model.csv). Default-entry filter gives one row per model (checked, no duplicates). Coverage of the 1,361: mutations 1,361; signatures 1,361 (Ploidy only 1,045); expression 1,292; **CN (WGS only) 1,221**; fusions 1,292 (RNA). 62 models have neither CN nor RNA.
- CN missingness is not random: 66% of the 140 CN-less models are Sanger-only (KY library) vs 18% of those with CN; lineage "Other" has none.
- **OmicsCNGeneWGS is a ratio, not log2**: quantiles 0 / 0.46 (1%) / 1.03 (median) / 1.57 (95%) / 255 (max), diploid ≈ 1, ploidy-relative (a WGD line with 2 of 4 copies reads ~0.5). DESIGN §2.1 thresholds (log2 < −1.2 etc.) are wrong for this file.
- Gene effects are model-level, jointly Chronos-integrated across libraries: 212 models have 2 libraries, 10 have ≥3. 1,334 genes have NaN in >1% of models, 909 in >10%, 648 in >50%. NaN is library-structured (high-NaN genes are NaN in ~75% of Avana-only models but ~19% of Humagne models). Part 1's 908 low-coverage genes are this set.
- Mutation matrices hold 0/1/2 (not binary). Damaging has 19.7k gene columns, 113 of which match CRISPR by Entrez but not by symbol. Hotspot has only 165 genes.
- Fusions: `CanonicalFusionName` uses `--` and arbitrary partner order (ABL1--BCR 13 models, BCR--ABL1 0). 43,613 distinct names; the commonest are self/readthrough/pseudogene artefacts (TFAM--TFAM 323). Counts by partner gene: KMT2A 26, EWSR1::FLI1 16, BCR::ABL1 13, PAX3::FOXO1 6, SS18::SSX 5 (SSX1 1 + SSX2 4). "≥5 models" yields 870 names, mostly junk. Models without RNA are unknown, not fusion-negative.
- Entrez works as the universal key: 18,434/18,434 CRISPR columns map to HGNC Approved (all have Ensembl; UniProt missing for 5, multi-accession for 39; 16 symbols differ). Entrez in `(…)` is stable, symbols are not.
- DEMETER2: 17,309 genes x 712 lines (CCLE-style names); 701 match Model.csv `CCLEName`, **549 are in CRISPRGeneEffect**; NaN 22.5%; 11 unmatched need alias mapping.
- 104 CRISPR models share a PatientID (1,305 distinct patients). Engineered/drug-adapted: 16 (matches design).
- MSIScore (signatures): 79 models ≥ 20, 86 ≥ 10 (DepMap convention MSI-H ≥ 20).
- Not present: `datasets/BioPlex/`, `Paralogs/` is empty, no proteomics, no ScreenSequenceMap (ScreenGeneEffect `SC-` ids cannot be linked to models or libraries).

## B. Required changes

### MUST
1. **CN definitions.** Problem: thresholds assume log2 CN; file is a ploidy-relative ratio with zeros and extreme values. Change: redefine on ratio r (deep deletion r < ~0.25-0.3 AND, where Ploidy exists, r x Ploidy < ~1; partial loss 0.5-0.9; amp r > 1.5 or absolute > ploidy+2), pre-register in DATA/DESIGN before any association, report calls where Ploidy is NaN (316 models) with a sensitivity "ratio only"; clip r at 10. Why: otherwise WGD lines are misclassified (CYCLOPS and MTAP/CDKN2A controls hinge on this). Also request `OmicsAbsoluteCNGene` (or arm-level calls) if the release has it.
2. **Context missingness is MNAR.** Problem: CN absent for 140 models (mostly Sanger/KY), RNA for 69. Change: per-test complete-case analysis, report n per test and which libraries are lost; never impute; add the "has-CN" indicator as a sensitivity covariate for CN contexts. Why: otherwise CN findings are partly library findings. Note (unverified, DepMap forum anecdote) that Chronos CN correction is weaker for models without WGS; run D5 on models with WGS only.
3. **Library/NaN handling in complex scores.** Problem: mean over observed members changes composition by library (NaN is library-defined), producing library-correlated scores. Change: (i) member inclusion rule fixed before testing: gene must be non-NaN in ≥95% of models, else dropped; complexes left with <3 members are not testable; (ii) library enters as multi-hot (not one-hot); (iii) report the fraction of members dropped per complex. Also use a robust score (trimmed mean or winsorised at ±3) since Chronos spans −6.2 to +5.5.
4. **Clusters built on the same data used for testing.** Problem: co-essential clusters form partly because of lineage/genotype-driven co-variation (SOX10/MITF, MYB-type modules). Context tests on all-data clusters are optimistic and "split-half replication" on fixed clusters is not independent. Change: for the headline claim, recompute zavg_BEF and clusters on discovery half A (150 s x 10 splits, fits the compute budget), freeze, test on half B; report all-data clusters as descriptive. Why: this is the one design flaw that would be raised by every reviewer.
5. **Fusion contexts.** Problem: design uses `::` names and a ≥5 rule. Change: define contexts as unordered, HGNC-protein-coding partner pairs from a pre-registered list (SS18::SSX1/2, EWSR1::FLI1, BCR::ABL1, KMT2A::any, PAX3/7::FOXO1) mapped via Ensembl ID → Entrez, excluding self-fusions and adjacent-gene readthroughs; combine with Model.csv `OncotreeCode`/`ModelSubtypeFeatures` (SYNS, ES, ARMS). Mark RNA-less models NA. Exploratory fusion discovery restricted to protein-coding partners and ≥8 models. Power: SS18::SSX n=5 is descriptive only.
6. **Novelty wording and comparator tables** (section C).

## C. Prior work: accuracy of novelty claims
- **Wainberg 2021** (Nat Genet 53:638): 485 lines confirmed; the claim "GLS + ACAT per module by cancer type, ClusterONE" and "442 modules / 16 cancer types" could not be verified (full text blocked): do not quote the numbers until checked. It is the closest competitor to D1; "2.8x more lines" is not a novelty claim.
- **Chen 2023** (Nat Commun, Chen X, Lu C; 1,086 lines, 145 modules): also integrated mutation, expression, epigenome and drug data and found PRC2/MLL-MEN1 in EZH2-mutant lymphoma. LITERATURE's "module vs cancer type" understates it; D3's genotype-to-module framing is not new in itself.
- **Camacho 2026** is a bioRxiv preprint (Feb 2026, doi 10.64898/2026.02.13.705694; Fallahi-Sichani lab), curated chromatin complexes, 42 lineages; cite as preprint.
- **Prindle 2025** (Nature 638:1095) has a companion, **Borck 2025** (Nature 638:1104): FOCAD (9p21.3) and TTC37/SKIC3 LoF destabilise the SKI complex. Use SKIC2/3/8 + FOCAD loss → PELO/HBS1L as a D3 positive control; "single-case precedent" is accurate but complex-level SL is a recognised concept.
- **Pan 2018**: RNAi-based, BAF/PBAF/ncBAF (GLTSCR1, BRD9, SMARCD1) module, confirmed. Add **Pan 2022** (sparse dictionary learning, pleiotropy) and Hassan 2023 as direct priors to D2: the claim "no systematic hierarchy-wide context-resolved sub-module analysis" is an absence claim; soften to "to our knowledge" or drop.
- **D5**: add Paolella 2017 (eLife, SF3B1, Achilles RNAi 179 lines) and Ryan 2017 (protein-complex collateral loss); a "CRISPR-era complex-aware CYCLOPS" absence is unverified. Since Chronos applies CN correction, a null CRISPR slope is uninterpretable; RNAi is the primary arm.
- Recent gene-level context SL work to cite: Lord 2020, Pacini 2024, SLAYER (NAR Genom Bioinform 2025), isogenic driver screens (Cell Rep 2026; not opened). Novelty must be restricted to the complex level with curation-free complexes.
