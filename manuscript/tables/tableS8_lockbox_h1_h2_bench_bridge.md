- H1: every event axis with its A estimate (κ_N for N, β_P for P; Chronos units) and its L estimate with 95% CI. Only the five non-neutral calls are eligible for confirmation; TP53 and PTEN carry the E2 label 'detectable, within equivalence margin' and cannot be promoted by a confirmation. EP300 P has 4 L loss lines and takes the rare-context route (leave-one-lineage-out, leave-one-line-out and DEMETER2 on A, all same sign); this is not an independent replication. FOCAD N failed in the primary (driver defect); its corrected confirmation is defect-sensitive and carries no claim.
- H2 (exploratory): confirmation by rank of the discovery's |t| among its own 20 random member splits (Table S5). Confirmation in L shows that a divergence replicates, not that it is sub-module-specific.
- BENCH: Δ = t_L(complex score) − t_L(best member g*, chosen in A), signed toward the A direction; 1,295 primary pairs in 353 complexes. Registered criterion: the complex wins if the high-breadth tercile CI excludes 0; it does not, so complex scores did not out-replicate the best gene. 16.5% of pair × replicate bootstrap fits fail and are dropped; with the covariate design assembled within each replicate, the high-tercile lower bound is −0.122 instead of −0.033 (independent review §5). The Spearman trend with breadth is reported without a p-value (pairs are not independent).
- BRIDGE-L: size-standardised lockbox confirmation rate of H3 discoveries made on the zavg map versus the GLS map (bootstrap B = 2,000, seed 5200). The R7 primary is the headline; the as-executed value is a disclosure. Every version favours GLS.
