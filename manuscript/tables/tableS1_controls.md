## Notes

- Sources: results/part2/controls/controls_summary.tsv, results/part2/controls/kill_rule.json (single run, results commit 4b5f800 after intent e82b657).
- Model: OLS of the unit score on the context plus registered covariates, HC3 standard error, Satterthwaite df; one-sided test in the registered direction at α = 0.05; A models only (n ≤ 1,007).
- β < 0 with alternative 'less' means more dependent (more negative Chronos effect) in the context. PC2 (context TP53-altered, expected dependency in TP53-WT) and PC5 (BRAF vs RAS hotspot, expected SHOC2 dependency in RAS) are coded so that the registered alternative is 'greater'.
- Level C = frozen complex unit (counted for the kill rule when evaluable); G = gene-level readout (reported, never counted).
- Kill rule (DESIGN §2.6): a direction is dropped if fewer than 50% of its evaluable complex-level controls are recovered.
- PC6 and PC12 reduce to single paralog genes under the registered paralog-component readout; only PC15 is a multi-gene H1 readout (independent review, results/part2/verify/controls_review.md).
